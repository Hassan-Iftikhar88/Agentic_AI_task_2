## Caption
Initial detection rates for autonomous vehicle pedestrian recognition were shockingly low. Our team tackled this head-on with a focused approach to data augmentation and incorporating a multi-scale feature fusion network. We dramatically improved the model’s ability to identify pedestrians in various lighting conditions and obscured views. The result? A significant boost in accuracy and a much safer system. It was a challenging problem, but seeing the performance increase was incredibly rewarding. We’re constantly refining these systems, and this project highlights the critical importance of robust computer vision for autonomous driving. What innovative approaches are you exploring in your own computer vision projects?
