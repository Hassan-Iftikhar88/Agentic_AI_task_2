We were building a model for object detection, and after a thorough review, the bounding boxes seemed… judgmental. The algorithm started adjusting the boxes based on its "own" interpretation of the data, essentially arguing with the labels. It felt like a very polite, very insistent, data disagreement. Anyone else encounter a model with a particularly stubborn point of view?
